What is this business, actually?

Strip away the excitement and the fear, and here is what somebody actually told you about. Three names — and each one has a job.

The company

NeoLife

A nutrition company founded in California in 1958 — 68 years selling real products in more than 50 markets, working in Nigeria since 2000. The products are the engine: families buy them, use them, finish them, and buy them again. That repeat buying is what pays commissions.

The team

FHG — Faitheroic Generation

A Nigerian team founded in 2016 in Ilesa, Osun State. FHG noticed something painful: good people were joining this business and quitting broke. So they turned the order upside down — train people first, free, then show them the business once money is no longer the wall.

This programme

Actimvated Network

The front door you were invited into: the free digital-skills training, the mentors who answer on WhatsApp, the centres spread across most states, and the monthly Cheque Rallies you may already have heard about. Everything on this site is run by real people you can reach today.

The honest part

Why did they build it this way?

Here is a truth many presenters skip: this business is real, but running it costs money. Registration. Monthly products. Time spent learning while your salary or hustle is still carrying you. Thousands of Nigerians entered with excitement and quit with empty pockets — not because the business failed them, but because their money finished before their skill arrived.

So FHG reversed the order. Learn a skill people pay for — free. Start earning from real projects. And only then, from your earnings and not your pocket, step into the NeoLife business if you choose. Same business. Different ending.

The questions you are already thinking.

We would rather answer them here — honestly — than have Google answer them for you.

"Is this not one of those scams?"

Fair question — and the answer is in facts, not feelings. NeoLife has traded since 1958 (68 years), sells physical products people actually use, and publishes its compensation plan openly. No Nigerian regulator has ever listed NeoLife as a scam.

And this programme? The training is free, so nobody collects your money and vanishes. What we will never do is promise you income — your results depend on your work, and we say so everywhere.

"I don't have money to start."

Then you are exactly who this system was built for. The training is ₦0 — start today without paying anything. As your skills grow, you move toward projects and earnings.

If you later choose distributorship, it has its own costs — and we show you every figure in black and white before you decide. From earnings, not from your pocket. That is the whole design.

"I don't want to beg my friends."

Good. Don't. This site exists precisely so you never have to pitch anybody. Send your friend this one link — it explains the model, plays the leaders' videos, and answers the scam question better than you could under pressure.

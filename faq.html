Questions people ask

Ask. It is allowed.

The ten questions we hear most — answered with facts, not excitement. If yours is not here, the contact button is one tap away.

1. "Is this not one of those scams?"

Fair question — and the answer is in facts, not feelings. NeoLife has traded since 1958 (68 years), sells physical products people actually use, and publishes its compensation plan openly. No Nigerian regulator — neither SEC nor FCCPC — has ever listed NeoLife as a scam.

And this programme? The training is free, so nobody collects your money and vanishes. What we will never do is promise you income — your results depend on your work, and we say so everywhere on this site.

2. "I saw the news about fake job adverts."

You saw right — and we will not defend it. Some people misused this business's name with fake job adverts. PUNCH investigated it, and NeoLife suspended those distributorships and supported police action. The company's own statement: it offers no jobs — it partners with independent distributors.

So let us say it plainly, as a standing rule: this is a business, not a job. Anyone who promises you "salary" here is lying to you — including if that person is from our team. Walk away from them.

3. "I heard a university banned this thing."

True, and we will not hide it: a university banned on-campus recruitment activities in 2026 because some recruiters behaved badly — pressure, odd-hour meetings. That was a conduct failure by people, not an illegality of the company.

Our team's rules are the opposite: education first, no pressure, no hiding. "Take your time" is a literal instruction here, not a sales trick. If you are a student, your studies come first — always.

4. "I don't have money to start."

Then you are exactly who this system was built for. The training is ₦0 — you start today without paying anything. As your skills grow, you move toward projects and earnings.

If you later choose distributorship, it has its own registration and monthly product costs — and we show you every figure in black and white before you decide. From earnings, not from your pocket. That is the whole design.

5. "I'm still a student."

Perfect timing, actually. Daramola Gabriel reached 1 Diamond Director as a part-5 student of Obafemi Awolowo University — with lectures, exams, and everything else you are facing.

Your timetable becomes your advantage: learn a skill between lectures, earn from it on your own hours, and grow at the pace your books allow. School first, always — the system flexes around that.

6. "People join, quit, and call it a scam."

Most people quit when their money finishes before their skill arrives — we showed you that wall on The Business page. The Member → Pro → Distributor path exists so it never happens to you: training first, income second, business third.

And when somebody quits and says "it doesn't work", ask them one question: "how many months did you work it?" The leaders on this site worked it for years — in public, on video.

7. "I don't want to beg my friends."

Good. Don't. This site exists precisely so you never have to pitch anybody. Send your friend the link — it explains the model, plays the leaders' videos, and answers the scam question better than you could under pressure.

You only do two things: welcome them, and connect them to a mentor. The site does the heavy lifting — the same way, every time. See our Invite Someone page for ready-made messages.

8. "How much can I really earn?"

Honest answer: nobody can promise you a figure, and whoever does is not being straight with you. What we can show you is the structure — NeoLife's published ranks and requirements — and the documented journeys of the leaders on our Testimonies page, including the years it took each of them.

Your income will depend on your effort, your consistency, and your season. Treat it like a real business and it can pay like one; treat it like a lottery and it will disappoint you like one.

9. "What exactly happens after I message on WhatsApp?"

A real person replies — usually the same day. No bot, no form that disappears into the internet. You will be asked your name and your location so a mentor near you can guide you.

From there: you can ask anything, book the next free training, or get your centre's Cheque Rally date. There is no payment and no pressure at any point. See our Contact page for the three easiest ways to start the chat.

10. "My family will say no."

Many of the leaders on this site heard exactly that. Samson Alani was doubted even at home in Ilesa. Their answer was not argument — it was consistency, and eventually, the official recognition videos.

Our suggestion: do not fight your family. Show them this website. Let the founders, the 68-year-old company, and the people doing it do the talking. Then take your time — the road is not running away from you.

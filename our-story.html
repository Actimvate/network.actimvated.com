Our Story

Born in Ilesa. Built for people like you.

Every system has an origin story. Ours begins with a founder who kept watching good people quit a good business — and decided to rebuild the road so that would stop happening.

2016 · Ilesa, Osun State

The wall he kept seeing

In 2016, Jagunmolu Abdulfatai kept seeing the same painful pattern around him. People he knew — teachers, artisans, fresh graduates — would join network marketing with fire in their eyes. For a few months they were loud about it. Then, slowly, they went quiet. No more invitations. No more excitement. Just that embarrassed look when somebody asked, "How is that your business going?"

He asked enough of them to learn the truth: it was not laziness, and it was not that the business was fake. Their money finished before their skill arrived. Registration had taken their savings, monthly products had eaten their salary, and by the time the business should have started paying them, the pocket was already empty.

He drew one conclusion that now powers everything you will read on this site: if money is the wall, then remove the money problem first.

The answer

So FHG was born

Faitheroic Generation — FHG — started in Ilesa with one rule that had never been tried around there: nobody gets shown the business until the system has first taught them a skill that pays. Free.

The rule

Train first. Free.

Every newcomer starts by learning practical digital skills — graphic design, websites, video, marketing — at zero cost. Learning comes before earning, and earning comes before the business.

The shape

A team, not a crowd

FHG grew as chapters — centres in towns and cities, mentors who answer questions personally, training rooms full of people at the same stage. You are never handed a link and abandoned.

The reach

From one town to most states

From that first Ilesa training room, FHG's free training has reached young people across 36 states of Nigeria (FHG-reported) — which is why the Cheque Rally date is different from location to location.

The company we build with

NeoLife — the 68-year-old engine

A team is only as trustworthy as the company behind it. FHG chose NeoLife, and the choice is one of the strongest arguments for this whole system.

1958

Where it began

Founded in California as GNLD, NeoLife has traded for 68 years and works in more than 50 markets. Companies that disappoint people do not collect anniversaries like that.

2000

Nigeria since

NeoLife has been part of Nigerian homes for over two decades — long enough for grandmothers to swear by the products and for generations of distributors to build real careers.

1976

Science since

A Scientific Advisory Board has guided NeoLife's formulations since 1976. The flagship, Tre-en-en Grain Concentrates, has been feeding families for decades.

2025

The cheques

NeoLife announced ₦31 billion paid to Nigerian distributors in 2025 — a company-wide total reported by PUNCH. A total, not a promise. But proof the money is real and moving.

Why we say names

Nothing about us is hidden

Most presentations like this one hide the company name and the team name until the last minute. We do the opposite — because a business that hides is already telling on itself.

You already know the names

FHG. NeoLife. Actimvated Network. Say them to anybody, search them anywhere, ask about them at any centre. Secrecy is how bad schemes survive; we cannot afford your confusion.

We show you the criticism too

When PUNCH investigated misuse of this business's name with fake job adverts, we did not remove the story — we linked it from our own footer. Read it yourself. Then read the company's response, and ask us anything about it.

We state the costs up front

The training is free. Distributorship, if you ever choose it, has real costs — and we show every figure before you decide, never after. That single rule would have saved most of the people the founder watched quit.

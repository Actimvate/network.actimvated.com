Your first step

FHG Day 1 — the orientation

Before anybody talks to you about distributorship, there is Day 1: a free orientation session where you see the whole system — the training, the earning path, the business, the team — and ask every question you have.

What it is

A guided first session for brand-new members: how the two-in-one model works, what the free training covers, and where it can lead. Nothing is sold on Day 1.

What to bring

Yourself, your phone if you like, and your questions — including the hard ones. Mentors answer them on the spot. Bring a skeptical friend too; they will enjoy it.

What it costs

Nothing. Day 1 is free, like the training itself. You leave knowing exactly what you would be joining — or you leave knowing this is not for you. Both are fine outcomes.

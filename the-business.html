The Business

How people in this business really earn.

Plain words. No stage, no music, no pressure. Just the mechanics — what you would actually do, what it actually costs, and where the money actually comes from.

First things first

What is network marketing, actually?

Forget everything you have heard — good and bad. Here is the machine, described simply.

Part 1

A company makes products

NeoLife makes nutrition, weight-management, home-care and skin-care products. Instead of paying supermarkets and billboards first, the company shares its selling budget with everyday people.

Part 2

People share, and earn

Registered distributors buy at member prices, sell at retail prices, and earn commission on products their customers reorder every month. That repeat buying is the engine of every cheque.

Part 3

Teams make it bigger

When you build a team of fellow distributors, you also earn on the volume moving through your team. This is the part people call "recruitment" — but the money legally comes from product movement, not from joining fees.

The model itself is not the scandal — it has existed since 1958 and is used by companies all over the world. The scandal is what bad presenters do on top of it: hiding costs and promising salaries. That is exactly what this system refuses to do.

The honest part

The wall that knocks most people down

If somebody had shown you only the cheques, this section would feel uncomfortable. Good. We would rather you see it now than feel it later.

You pay to enter

Distributorship has registration and a starter product order. For many hardworking people, that first payment alone is a hard decision.

You pay monthly

Keeping your distributor account active means servicing it with products month after month — before your income has grown legs of its own.

You pay with time

Learning, events, meetings — all real hours, spent while your salary or hustle is still carrying your life.

So the quit comes

Not because the person is lazy. Because the pocket gave up first — and every quit writes one more "it doesn't work" story in the neighbourhood.

Now, our fix. What if the system itself carried those costs — from income it helped you create first?

Straight talk

What this business is not

Any presenter who cannot say these four sentences out loud is not safe to follow. We say them out loud.

Not a job

Nobody here will offer you a salary. NeoLife itself says it offers no jobs — it partners with independent distributors. If anybody promises you pay, walk away from them.

Not quick money

Every leader on this site worked for years. Ask anybody flashing a cheque how long they worked it — then compare their answer to their story.

Not a fee-collecting scheme

Money legally comes from products people buy and finish, not from joining fees. That is why our products page matters as much as our income page.

Not for under-18s

Distributorship is for adults (18+). Learning, though, is open to every age — students included, once school comes first.
